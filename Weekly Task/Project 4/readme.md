# Project 4: Ethical Considerations in Deep Learning & AI

## Overview
An analytical report examining the critical ethical, legal, and societal dimensions of developing and deploying deep learning models in real-world scenarios.

## Key Focus Areas
- **Algorithmic Bias & Fairness**: How biased historical training data perpetuates discriminatory outcomes in hiring, lending, and criminal justice.
- **Data Privacy & Surveillance**: Protection of sensitive personal information, consent mechanisms, and federated learning concepts.
- **Transparency & Explainability (XAI)**: Addressing the "black-box" dilemma in mission-critical applications like medical diagnostics and autonomous driving.
- **Accountability & Governance**: Establishing liability frameworks and regulatory compliance for automated decision-making systems.
